## Confirmed blockers and verified fixes

1. **`P2-DPP-DETERMINISM` (review `94059344` finding 1). Nondeterministic DPP passage ordering across processes on varied-frequency symmetric vectors.**
   At `3abfad1`, `native/src/sag2/mod.rs` accumulated TF-IDF norms (`norm_sq`) and pairwise sparse dot products (`dot`) over `std::collections::HashMap` iteration order. Per-map random SipHash seeds caused tiny floating-point rounding deltas that flipped strict residual comparisons in Cholesky DPP. Reproducing the varied-frequency five-passage probe across 60 fresh release CLI processes yielded 14 distinct full passage orders (`.audit/before-repro.json`), while the unit regression `dpp_varied_frequency_symmetric_vectors_are_deterministic` failed on its first run (`[0, 4, 2, 1, 3]` vs `[0, 1, 2, 3, 4]`) and `group7_dpp_varied_frequency_determinism_across_processes` diverged at run 1.
   **Resolution.** `native/src/sag2/mod.rs` replaces `HashMap` in `TermDfCache`, `pool0_by_id`, `derive_expansion_terms`, and `rank_dpp_indices` with `BTreeMap` and sorted sparse `Vec<(String, f64)>` vectors paired with `sorted_sparse_dot` two-pointer merging. Both `dpp_varied_frequency_symmetric_vectors_are_deterministic` (40 in-process repeats) and `group7_dpp_varied_frequency_determinism_across_processes` (20 fresh CLI processes) pass, and the 60-process release CLI probe produces 1 identical full order across 60 runs.

2. **`P2-PRE-IO-OUTPUT-BUDGET` (review `94059344` finding 2). Impossible positive `--max-bytes` values execute store bootstrap and spend model HTTP requests before failing.**
   At `3abfad1`, `native/src/cli.rs` rejected only `--max-bytes 0` up front and deferred `fit_sag2_search_budget` until after `authorize_search_mode`, `ensure_current_store`, and `run_sag2`. Running `clec search anchor --sag2 --max-bytes 1` sent 1 loopback scoring POST (`httpAttempts: 1`, `roundsCompleted: 1`) before exiting with code 2 and `OUTPUT_BUDGET_TOO_SMALL` (`.audit/before-repro.json`).
   **Resolution.** `native/src/sag2/mod.rs` exposes `minimum_sag2_search_data` and `native/src/cli.rs` computes `minimum_sag2_search_envelope_bytes` for `cli.sag2` right after `prepare_search_request` and before `authorize_search_mode` or `ensure_current_store` (preserving plain local Stage 4 bootstrap behavior). The lower bound uses exact `queries` and `matchMode`, `"0".repeat(64)` release, `"a"` requested/actual model, `"completed"` stop reason, `0` integer counters, `"unknown"` metadata statuses with `Some(0)` tokens and `Some(0.0)` cost (3 JSON bytes vs 4 for `null`), empty `hits` and `omittedIds`, and CLI `max_bytes`. Unit test `minimum_search_envelope_is_conservative_lower_bound_across_modes` and real-CLI test `group1_tiny_positive_max_bytes_rejected_before_io` verify pre-I/O rejection (exit 2, empty stdout, `sag2Stage: null`, 0 HTTP requests, cold `--data-dir` uncreated, unreadable `--key-file` unopened) and preserve exact-fit and one-under final `output_budget` trimming.

3. **`P2-PROTOCOL-COVERAGE-GAPS` (review `94059344` finding 3). Advertised Phase 5 verification cases lacked assertions or had vacuous negative controls.**
   At `3abfad1`, `native/tests/sag2_protocol.rs` omitted `--work`/`--source`/`--date-to` Round 1 request-log verification, seen-ID exclusion for new expansion candidates, authored round-robin and term-tie order, 4-query fusion, pre/post default baseline parity against the accepted Stage 4 binary, reachable cap boundaries (`32` attempts, `4096`/`4097` DF lookups, `32,000`/`64,000` token estimates, `24` SQL queries), and used `assert_ne!(srv_reverse.logs().len(), 99)` in group 10 while overclaiming structurally unreachable ceilings.
   **Resolution.** Added `group7_filters_seen_id_round_robin_term_ties_and_four_query_fusion`, `group7_default_local_baseline_parity_with_pre_sag2_release` (against `/Users/century/.t3/worktrees/clec/t3-c4c9bebd-0d1d9b692d1e433e/native/target/release/clec`, SHA-256 `d41980be0bbee34065f58f90d7cbb06193384949aa412eae95217a1737da3bcc`), `group8_exact_reachable_caps_32_attempts_df4097_and_token_estimates`, and tightened `group10_negative_controls_fail_for_predicted_reasons` with isolated Round 1 score reversal and a real extra-request failure against `assert_exact_request_count`. Narrowed `evaluation/clec-v1/phase5/PROTOCOL.md` and `docs/agent-work/clec-native/phase5-report.md` with explicit traceability and algebraic proofs of structurally dominated caps.

4. **`P2-RESPONSE-BYTES-ON-FAILURE` (review `faad9103` finding 1). Failed response streams report `responseBytes: 0`.**
   At `3abfad1`, `native/src/sag2/jev.rs` updated `self.response_bytes` only after `TransferEvent::Finished(Ok(()))`. A 65,536-byte partial stream closing before `Content-Length: 100000` exited 4 with `responseBytes: 0`, and a lengthless 131,073-byte stream exited 5 with `responseBytes: 0`.
   **Resolution.** `execute_single_post` in `native/src/sag2/jev.rs` adds each observed `TransferEvent::Chunk` length to `self.response_bytes` immediately upon receipt (including the overflow sentinel chunk) before checking per-response (`MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES`) or total (`MAX_TOTAL_RESPONSE_BYTES`) limits, removing the post-completion add to prevent double counting. `group4_observed_response_bytes_on_failure_and_exact_128k` and `group8_fixture_dns_setup_cleanup_stalls_and_named_tls_sni` verify `responseBytes: 65536` on partial close and timeout-after-prefix, `responseBytes: 131073` on lengthless oversize, `responseBytes: 0` on header `Content-Length` rejection, and `responseBytes: 131072` on exact-at success.

5. **`P2-HEADER-CAP-ACCOUNTING` (review `faad9103` finding 2). Request header admission omitted generated `Host`, `Content-Length`, `Accept`, and HTTP/1.1 framing.**
   At `3abfad1`, `validate_secret_and_headers` in `native/src/sag2/jev.rs` summed only four explicit field names and values, accepting an 8,089-byte key that produced 8,256 serialized HTTP/1.1 header bytes on the wire.
   **Resolution.** `canonical_http1_header_block_bytes` and `canonical_host_header_value` in `native/src/sag2/jev.rs` account for all seven generated headers (`authorization`, `content-type`, `accept-encoding`, `content-length`, `accept`, `user-agent`, `host`) plus `30` HTTP/1.1 framing bytes (`": "` + `"\r\n"` per header and terminating `"\r\n"`). `validate_secret_and_headers` enforces the `8192`-byte limit pre-bootstrap using a 1-digit `Content-Length` lower bound (bounding `Host` and credential sizes before `--data-dir` creation), and `reserve_attempt` re-validates exact `batch.body.len()` `Content-Length` digits before incrementing `http_attempts` or dispatching. `group4_header_cap_includes_generated_headers_and_framing` proves cold host-overflow pre-bootstrap rejection, exact `8192` wire-header pass, `8193` one-over pre-dispatch rejection, `8089`-byte key rejection, and 5-digit `Content-Length` payload-transition rejection.

6. **`P2-USAGE-OVERFLOW` (review `faad9103` finding 3). Multi-batch usage and cost aggregation saturated integer tokens and serialized infinite float cost as `null` with `costStatus: "reported"`.**
   At `3abfad1`, `execute_single_post` used `saturating_add` on `total_input_tokens` and `total_output_tokens`, added `cost_usd` without checking finiteness, and incremented `successful_batches` before validating usage aggregation.
   **Resolution.** `execute_single_post` in `native/src/sag2/jev.rs` computes checked `u64::checked_add` sums for `input_tokens` and `output_tokens`, validates `total.is_finite()` for `cost_usd`, preserves `None` propagation across partial batches (`usage_seen_all.unwrap_or(true)`), and fails closed with `ErrorCode::JevInvalidResponse` (exit 5) before committing `successful_batches`, `actual_model`, or aggregate usage/cost state while preserving spent `http_attempts` and observed `response_bytes`. `group4_usage_and_cost_overflow_fail_closed` covers `input_tokens` overflow, `output_tokens` overflow, `cost` overflow, valid `u64::MAX` boundary sums, missing-usage propagation, and `null`-cost propagation.
